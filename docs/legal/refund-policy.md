# Refund Policy — underground

**TEMPLATE — review with legal counsel before publishing. Last updated: 2026-06-13.**

This policy reflects how refunds actually work on the platform (escrow-backed).

## When you can get a refund
- **Before funds clear.** Money for a paid ticket sits in escrow as *held*, then *clearing* after
  the event ends. While an order is `held` or `clearing` you can:
  - **Return your ticket** yourself (*My tickets → Return*) — this frees the seat and refunds you,
    provided the funds have not yet cleared.
  - **Open a dispute** (*Order → Dispute*) — this freezes the escrow so the organizer cannot be
    paid until an admin resolves it.
- **After funds clear or are paid out**, self-service refunds are no longer available — the
  organizer is owed/paid. Contact support; refunds then depend on the organizer.

## Admin-resolved disputes
An admin can **refund** a disputed order (money back, ticket returned, seat freed, any promo use
restored) or **release** it to the organizer. A frozen/disputed order is never released silently —
release of a disputed order requires an explicit override.

## Free events
Free reservations can be returned at any time before the event; no money is involved.

## How refunds are issued
Refunds are returned to the original Lemon Squeezy payment method. Processing time depends on the
payment provider and your bank.

## Requesting help
Use the in-app dispute flow first. For anything else, contact **support@underground.app** with
your order number.
