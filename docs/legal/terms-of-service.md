## Accounts
You must provide accurate information and keep your credentials secure. You must be old enough to
form a binding contract. Accounts that violate these terms may be blocked; a blocked account loses
access immediately.

## Buying tickets
- Prices are shown per event in the event's currency. Promo codes, where offered, are applied at
  checkout and **consumed at payment**.
- Some events carry an **age restriction**; you must meet the minimum age (verified via your date
  of birth) to buy or reserve a ticket.
- A ticket is a revocable license to attend, subject to the organizer's rules. Tickets are
  single-use (validated by QR at entry).

## Escrow & organizer payouts
Funds for a paid ticket are held in **escrow** when you buy. They move to *clearing* after the
event ends and *clear* after a dispute-free window (default 5 days). Organizers are paid out only
after funds clear **and** the organizer has passed KYC verification. This protects buyers from
"collect-and-disappear" organizers.

## Organizer obligations
Organizers must hold the events they list, honor sold tickets, provide accurate age ratings, and
complete KYC (identity + bank details) before receiving payouts. We deduct a platform commission
from payouts.

## Disputes
Buyers may open a dispute on an order while funds are still recallable; this freezes the escrow
until an admin resolves it (refund or release). See the Refund Policy.
