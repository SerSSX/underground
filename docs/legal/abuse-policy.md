# Abuse & Content Reporting Policy — underground

**TEMPLATE — review with legal counsel before publishing. Last updated: 2026-06-13.**

We want underground to be safe. This policy explains what's not allowed and how to report it.

## Prohibited
- Illegal activity, fraud, or scam events.
- Harassment, hate speech, threats, or doxxing.
- Sexual content involving minors (zero tolerance — reported to authorities).
- Spam, impersonation, or intellectual-property infringement.
- Circumventing age restrictions or platform safeguards.

## How to report
Use the **Report** action available on events, profiles, posts, and reviews
(`POST /api/reports`). Tell us the target and a short reason; add details if helpful. Reports are
private and go to our moderation queue.

## What we do
Moderators review the queue (`open → reviewed → resolved`) and may hide or remove content, block
users, or freeze related escrow. A blocked account loses access immediately. Serious or illegal
content is escalated and, where required, reported to authorities.

## Age safety
Organizers must set an accurate age rating; buyers' ages are verified against their date of birth.
Misrepresenting an event's age rating is a policy violation.

## Appeals & contact
If you believe a moderation action was wrong, contact **trust@underground.app**.
